Our story

My name is Hatim Abu Tawila. I am 56 years old and from Gaza, Palestine. I am sharing my story in the hope of receiving support to help my wife and me build a more secure future.

During the war in Gaza, our home was completely destroyed. We lost the place where we had built our lives and the stability we once knew.

We also lost our only adopted son, who was killed during the war. His memory remains an important part of our lives as we look toward the future.

Today, my wife and I hope to find a way to rebuild our lives and access opportunities that have become difficult to pursue under our current circumstances.

How the funds will be used

These funds will help cover the costs associated with the following goals.

  1. 1

    Seeking safety

    We hope to use part of the funds to cover the expenses associated with leaving Gaza and reaching a safer place, subject to the necessary travel arrangements and permissions.

  2. 2

    Accessing medical care

    We hope to consult qualified medical professionals and explore appropriate medical advice and treatment options for infertility.

  3. 3

    Exploring fertility treatment

    My wife and I would also like to explore the possibility of having a child through in vitro fertilization (IVF), if recommended by qualified medical specialists. We understand that treatment outcomes cannot be guaranteed, but access to medical advice and care would give us an opportunity to consider our options.
